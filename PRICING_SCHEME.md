# We're Cured! — Pricing Scheme

A consistent method for pricing handmade resin pieces. Use this every time a new product
is priced so nothing is guessed and prices stay fair and consistent.

_Currency: **Canadian dollars (CAD)**. Default labour rate: **$25/hr** (adjust if Janet changes it)._

---

## The core formula

**Price = (Materials + Labour + Overhead) × Markup**

1. **Materials** — everything that goes into and around ONE piece:
   - Resin + hardener (cost per ounce × amount used)
   - Pigments / alcohol inks, glitter, foil, crystals, embellishments
   - Mold cost ÷ number of reuses
   - Finishing: cork/felt backing, varnish, sandpaper
   - **Packaging**: box, tissue, and the branded stickers / gift tags / thank-you cards

2. **Labour** — hourly rate × *active* time (mixing, pouring, demolding, sanding,
   finishing, photographing — NOT the passive curing wait). Default rate **$25/hr**.

3. **Overhead / "oops" buffer** — electricity, tool wear, occasional failed pours.
   Add roughly **10–15%** of (Materials + Labour).

4. **Markup** — for handmade sold direct, multiply by **×2 to ×2.5**. This covers profit
   on top of overhead. Underpricing is the #1 beginner mistake — don't skip the markup.

**Worked examples:**
- Geode Set of 4: Materials $9 + Labour (1.5 hr × $25 = $37.50) = $46.50 → ×2.2 ≈ $102 → **round to $99**
- Simple single coaster: Materials $2 + Labour (0.4 hr × $25 = $10) = $12 → ×2.2 ≈ **$26**

## ⭐ Key principle: price by CATEGORY & perceived value, not by size or material

Small doesn't mean cheap. Jewellery is worn, personal, and giftable, so it carries a **premium** —
a tiny pendant can out-earn a big coaster. Buyers also compare *within a category* (a coaster vs
cheap mass-produced coasters → price pressure; a pendant vs other handmade necklaces → $40–80).
So always compare **like-for-like** (set-to-set, pendant-to-pendant), and let the category — not the
gram weight — lead the price.

## Tiers (category-based)

| Category | Examples | Rough band (CAD) |
|---|---|---|
| Earrings | studs, small drops | $20–40 |
| **Pendants** | worn jewellery — commands a premium for its size | **$45–70** |
| Coaster sets | set of 4, priced by complexity | $25–70 |
| Statement pieces | bowls, platters, "hands" sculptures | $60–200+ |

**Difficulty bump within a category:** plain pour ×1 · geode with crystal edges / intricate work ×1.3–1.6.

> Reality check (Etsy, 2026): handmade resin coasters start ~$15 (often *singles* or basic), while
> resin pendants reach ~$70. Don't chase the $15 coaster number — those aren't comparable to sets of 4
> with crystal edges. And don't be shy on pendants: the market supports jewellery pricing.

## Tips & reminders
- **Sanity-check** against Etsy comparables (e.g., "resin geode coasters set of 4"), but
  price at the **higher** end — the work is one-of-a-kind, local, and handmade.
- **Selling fees:** Etsy ≈ 6.5% + payment fees; own-site checkout ≈ 2.9% + 30¢. Bake into price.
- **Shipping:** resin is heavy and fragile — factor packaging + postage; decide free-shipping
  (priced in) vs. charged separately.
- **Round nicely** ($99, not $101.40). Consider **bundle deals**.

---

## Info needed to price a NEW product
When a new product is added, gather these before quoting a price:
1. Category / size tier (small, medium, large, statement)
2. Materials cost for that piece (resin amount + embellishments + mold share + finishing + packaging)
3. Active labour time in hours
4. Difficulty (plain vs. geode/intricate)
5. (Optional) a comparable market price to sanity-check
Then apply the formula and fit the result to the tier bands above.
