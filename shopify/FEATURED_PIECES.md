# Choosing the Featured pieces

The home page has a **Featured** row right under the welcome banner. You choose what goes in it
by putting a tag on a piece in Shopify. There's nothing to change on the website itself.

## How to feature a piece

**On the computer (Shopify admin):**
1. Go to **Products** and open the piece.
2. On the right-hand side, find the box called **Product organization**.
3. Click in the **Tags** field, type **Featured** and press **Enter**.
4. Click **Save**.

**On the phone (Shopify app):**
1. Tap **Products** and open the piece.
2. Scroll down to **Tags**, tap it, type **Featured** and tap **Add** (or Done).
3. Tap **Save**.

The website picks it up next time someone opens the page. You may need to refresh to see it yourself.

**To take a piece out of the row**, open it the same way and click the little **×** on the Featured
tag, then Save.

## Good to know

- **Up to 8 show.** Eight makes two neat rows of four on a computer. On a phone, people swipe
  sideways through them. If you tag more than 8, the most recently added ones show.
- **Fewer is fine too.** Four looks lovely as a single row.
- **Sold pieces drop out by themselves**, so the row never shows something people can't buy.
- **If nothing is tagged**, the row doesn't disappear. It turns into **New Arrivals** and shows your
  8 newest pieces instead.
- The tag doesn't care about capitals: Featured, featured or FEATURED all work.
- **The NEW badge is separate.** Any piece that went live in the last 30 days gets a little "NEW"
  label on its photo, wherever it shows. You don't tag anything for that; it takes care of itself.

## How often to change it

There's no rule. Some ideas:
- **When you add a new batch**, feature two or three of the best new pieces.
- **For the season**, like snowy or wintery pieces in December and flowers in spring.
- **When something sells**, it drops out on its own, so that's a natural moment to tag a replacement.
- **Pieces that haven't had much attention** deserve a turn in the spotlight.

Every two or three weeks is a nice rhythm. It keeps the home page fresh for people who come back,
without turning into a chore.
