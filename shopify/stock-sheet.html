This sheet is sitting inside the Claude page, and browsers won't let it start a print from there. Open it in its own tab — open the stock sheet — then press ⌘P (Ctrl+P on Windows). Whatever filter you've picked comes with it.

Simpler still: there's a copy saved on your Mac at website / werecured / shopify / stock-sheet.html — double-click that and the Print button just works.

Weighed column: write the packed weight in grams — the piece in its box, padded, ready to post. That's the number Shopify wants when you buy a label, not the bare piece.

Weights don't change what a customer is charged while you're on flat rates. They save you typing at the label counter, and they become compulsory if you ever move to calculated rates or add US shipping.

Quickest route: weigh one packed example per category, then bulk-edit that figure onto the rest of that type in Shopify. Close enough beats zero.
