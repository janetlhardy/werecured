Paste these into Shopify: **Settings → Policies.** For **Privacy policy** and **Terms of service**,
just click **"Insert template"** (Shopify's boilerplate is fine — the Privacy one covers Canada's PIPEDA).
Paste the two below into **Refund policy** and **Shipping policy**.

## Refund Policy

Every We're Cured! piece is handmade and one-of-a-kind, so **all sales are final** — we're unable to
accept returns or exchanges for change of mind. Because each item is made by hand, small variations in
colour, pattern, sparkle, and bubbles are natural characteristics of resin art, not defects, and
colours may appear slightly different on screen than in person.

**If your item arrives damaged:** We pack every order carefully, but resin can be fragile in transit.
If your piece arrives broken or damaged, please email us at **hello@werecured.ca within 3 days of
delivery** with a photo of the damaged item and a photo of the packaging it arrived in. Since each
piece is one-of-a-kind and can't be remade identically, we'll issue you a **full refund** for any item
that arrives damaged. Please keep the item and all original packaging until your claim is resolved.

**Wrong item:** If you received the wrong item, email **hello@werecured.ca** and we'll make it right.

Questions? Contact us at **hello@werecured.ca**.

## Shipping Policy

**Processing time:** Orders are packed and shipped within **3–5 business days**. You'll receive a
tracking number by email once your order is on its way.

**Where we ship:** We currently ship within **Canada** via Canada Post. Shipping is a flat rate based
on the type of piece (jewellery, coasters, or larger home décor items) and is shown at checkout.

**Local pickup:** Free local pickup is available at checkout for local customers — just select
"Local pickup" at checkout.  *(← delete this line if you're not offering local pickup)*

**Careful packaging:** Resin pieces are fragile, so every order is wrapped with generous padding to
arrive safely.

**Damaged in transit:** In the rare case a piece arrives damaged, please see our Refund Policy — email
**hello@werecured.ca** within 3 days of delivery with photos and we'll take care of you.

**Delivery times:** Once shipped, delivery estimates are provided by Canada Post and aren't guaranteed.
We're not responsible for carrier delays, but we're always happy to help you track a package.

**Shipping address:** Please double-check your address at checkout — we're unable to cover reshipping
costs for incorrectly entered addresses.
