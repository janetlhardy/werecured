# We're Cured! — Craft Show / Shopify POS Guide

How to sell in person at markets and craft shows using **Shopify POS**, so your in-person and online
sales share **one inventory** (sell a piece at a show → it shows "Sold" on the website automatically).

_This file is kept off the public website._

---

## What you need
- **Shopify POS app** — free (POS Lite is included in your plan). Install on your phone or tablet.
- **A way to take cards** — pick one:
  - **Tap to Pay on iPhone** — *free*, no hardware; accept tap/Apple Pay right on a recent iPhone.
  - **Tap & Chip reader** — ~$49 CAD; small Bluetooth reader for tap + chip. Order from Shopify admin → Settings → (search) Hardware, or shopify.com/pos.
  - **POS Go** — ~$180+ CAD; all-in-one handheld with its own screen (nicest for busy tables).

---

## One-time setup (do at home before your first show)
1. **Install** the **Shopify POS** app (App Store / Google Play) and **log in**
   (Google account: **janethardy@werecured.ca**).
2. **Publish products to Point of Sale.** In Shopify admin: **Products → select all →
   make available on the *Point of Sale* channel** (same idea as the Headless/website channel).
   Only products on this channel show up in the POS app.
3. **Set up your card method:**
   - iPhone: turn on **Tap to Pay on iPhone** in the POS app settings, OR
   - Pair your **Tap & Chip reader** (POS app → Settings → Hardware → connect).
4. **Do a practice sale** — ring up one item and either use test mode or make a $1 real sale you
   refund, so you're comfortable with the flow.

---

## At the show
1. Open the **Shopify POS** app.
2. **Add items to the cart** — tap products from your list (search by name).
3. Tap **Charge / Checkout**.
4. Take payment:
   - **Card:** customer taps or inserts their card.
   - **Cash:** tap "Cash," enter amount, it calculates change.
5. Offer a **receipt** (email or text) — optional but nice, and it grows your customer list.
6. Inventory updates itself — the piece is now marked sold everywhere.

---

## Practical tips
- 📶 **Internet:** show Wi-Fi is often bad. Bring a **phone hotspot** or use cellular — card payments
  need a connection.
- 🔋 **Charge everything** the night before; bring a power bank.
- 💵 **Cash float:** bring small bills/coins for change if you'll take cash.
- 🏷️ Keep clear **price tags** on pieces so customers (and you) can ring up quickly.
- 🧾 After the show, review your sales in **Shopify admin → Orders / Analytics.**

---

## Fees
- In-person card sales: a small processing fee (~2.7%).
- Cash sales: no fee (you just record them).

## Local pickup (bonus)
Since you're local and do markets, you can offer **free local pickup** as a checkout option on the
website — great for nearby customers who'd rather grab it in person than pay shipping. Ask Claude to
help set it up when you're ready.
