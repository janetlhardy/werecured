# We're Cured! — Shipping Setup Guide

How to charge shipping fairly across very different products (tiny light pendants → heavy compact
coaster sets → light bulky bowls) — and actually get parcels out the door cheaply.

_This file is kept off the public website. Start Canada-only; add the US later once comfortable._

---

## The big idea
Two separate things:
1. **What you CHARGE the customer** at checkout (your shipping rates).
2. **How you SHIP it** (buy a discounted label, pack it, mail it).

On the **Basic plan**, live "calculated" rates (real weight+size+distance quotes at checkout) aren't
included — they need the Advanced plan or a paid add-on (~$20/mo, often free on annual billing). So we
charge our **own rates**, and use **shipping profiles** to price different product groups fairly.

---

## Recommended setup (works on Basic)

### Step 1 — Shipping profiles (group products by how they ship)
Shopify admin → **Settings → Shipping and delivery**. Create these groups:

| Profile | Products | Suggested rate (within Canada) |
|---|---|---|
| **Jewellery** | Pendants, Earrings | **Free** (bake ~$4 into the price) or flat **$5** |
| **Coasters** | All coaster sets/singles | Flat **$12** (heavy but compact) |
| **Large & fragile** | Bowls, Platters, Sculptures/"Hands" | Flat **$20** (light but big box) |

Grouping by profile is what fixes the "coaster vs bowl" problem — you price each by how it *really*
ships, not by weight alone. (Numbers above are starting points — adjust once you see real label costs.)

### Step 2 — Free local pickup
Add **Local pickup** as an option (Settings → Shipping → Local pickup). Great for your market/local
customers — they skip shipping entirely.

### Step 3 — Buy discounted labels to ship (all plans)
When an order comes in: **Orders → the order → Buy shipping label** → **Canada Post** (discounted).
Print at home, pack, done. (UPS labels also available if cheaper for a given parcel.)

## Go shopping — packaging list 🛍️
- **Bubble mailers** (padded envelopes), ~6×9 in — for **jewellery**.
- **Small sturdy boxes**, ~6×6×3 in or 8×8×3 in — for **coaster sets** (they're heavy, so strong boxes).
- **Medium/large boxes**, ~10×10×5 in and ~12×12×6 in — for **bowls & platters** (need room for padding).
- **Bubble wrap + packing paper/peanuts** — resin is beautiful but *breakable*; pad generously.
- **"Fragile" stickers**, packing tape, a fine scale (kitchen scale is fine for weighing pieces).
- Optional: **We're Cured! stickers / thank-you cards / tissue** for a branded unboxing.

Tell Shopify your box sizes: **Settings → Shipping → Packages** → add the 2–3 boxes you actually use.

---

## Set product weights (do this once)
We imported everything at **weight = 0**. Even for flat rates it's good to fill these in (and it's
required if you ever move to calculated rates). Use the **bulk editor** (like you did for prices) and
weigh a finished piece of each type. Rough starting weights *with packaging*:

| Item | Rough shipped weight |
|---|---|
| Pendant / earrings | 60–120 g |
| Single coaster | 250–400 g |
| Coaster set of 4 | 900 g – 1.5 kg |
| Bowl / platter | 500 g – 1.2 kg (but bulky!) |

## Fulfilling an order (what to do when a sale comes in) 🎉
You'll get an **email**, and the order appears in **Shopify admin → Orders**.

1. **Open the order** → check the items and the shipping address.
2. **Pack it:**
   - Pick the right box/mailer for that product group.
   - Pad generously (bubble wrap / paper) — resin is fragile.
   - Add branded touches: **thank-you card, sticker, tissue**.
   - Seal it and add a **"Fragile"** sticker for bowls/platters.
3. **Weigh the packed parcel** (kitchen scale) — you'll enter this for the label.
4. **Buy the label:** in the order, click **Buy shipping label → Canada Post** → choose the service
   (Regular Parcel is usually cheapest; Expedited is faster) → confirm the **box size and weight** →
   **Buy label.** The discounted cost bills to your Shopify account.
5. **Print** the label, tape it on.
6. Buying the label **marks the order fulfilled** and **adds tracking automatically** — your customer
   gets a shipping email. ✨
7. **Drop off** at a Canada Post outlet (post office, or the counter in many Shoppers Drug Marts), or
   schedule a pickup.

**Tips**
- Ship promptly (aim for 1–3 business days) — buyers love fast handling.
- Keep packing supplies **stocked ahead** so a sale is never stressful.
- For pricey pieces, consider **adding insurance** when buying the label.
- Double-check the **address** before printing.
- Snap a quick photo of packed fragile items — handy if a shipping claim ever comes up.

## Shipping jewellery cheaply by Lettermail (no Shopify label) 💎✉️
Shopify only sells Canada Post **parcel** labels (~$12 min), which is far too much for light
jewellery. Ship those by **Lettermail** at the post office instead — the customer pays your **$5 flat
jewellery rate**, and postage only costs ~$2–4, so you come out ahead.

**Steps when a jewellery order comes in:**
1. Open the order in Shopify and note the customer's **shipping address**.
2. **Hand-address a thin padded envelope** (customer address + your return address). Tuck in a
   thank-you card / sticker.
3. **Buy Lettermail postage at the post office** (or use stamps) — just ask for "lettermail," ~$2–4.
4. In Shopify, on the order, click **"Mark as fulfilled"** *instead of* "Buy shipping label" →
   **leave the tracking number blank** → **Fulfill.** (The customer still gets a "shipped" email.)

**Keep in mind:**
- Lettermail must be **under 2 cm thick** and **under 500 g**. Flat earrings = fine; a **chunky
  pendant may be too thick** → that one has to go as a parcel (pricier label).
- **No tracking** = no proof of delivery. **Snap a photo of the sealed, addressed envelope** before
  mailing, as insurance against "it never arrived" claims.
- Coasters, bowls, and home décor still ship as normal **parcels** with a Shopify Canada Post label.

_Later option: a discount broker like **Chit Chats** or **Stallion Express** offers cheap **tracked**
small-parcel rates — worth exploring if jewellery sells well and you want tracking._

---

## When to upgrade to live calculated rates
If shipping variance starts costing you (lots of far-away orders on the heavy items), you can unlock
real weight+size+distance quotes at checkout by **switching to Shopify annual billing** (often unlocks
it free) or moving to the **Advanced plan**. No rush — flat profiles + local pickup are a solid start.
