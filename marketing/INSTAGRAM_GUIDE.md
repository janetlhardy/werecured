# We're Cured! — Instagram Starter Kit

A plain-language guide for growing **@were_cured** and sending people to **werecured.ca**.
You don't need to do everything at once. Start with the **First Week Checklist** at the bottom.

_This file is kept off the public website._

---

## The goal (keep it simple)
Instagram does two jobs for you:
1. **Get discovered** by people who've never heard of you — mostly through **Reels** (short videos).
2. **Turn watchers into buyers** — by making it easy to find and trust your shop.

You don't need to go viral. A steady trickle of the *right* people finding your work is what grows sales.

---

## 1. Your bio (do this once)
Instagram has a few **separate fields** — put the right text in each:

**Name field** (the bold line, max 30 characters — this one is searchable):
> We're Cured! ✨ Resin Art

**Bio text** (the description, max 150 characters):
> Handmade resin coasters, jewellery & décor 🌈
> One-of-a-kind • Made in BC 🇨🇦
> Glow-in-the-dark pieces that really glow 🌙
> Shop new arrivals ↓

_Trimmed version if it's over the 150-char limit (emojis count extra):_
> Handmade resin coasters, jewellery & décor 🌈
> One-of-a-kind • Made in BC 🇨🇦
> Glow pieces that really glow 🌙
> Shop below ↓

**Website field** (the only clickable link on Instagram — point it at the shop):
> werecured.ca

**Tips**
- The `↓` arrows point down to the website link.
- Switch to a free **Professional / Business account** (Settings → Account type and tools) for a
  category label, contact button, and insights.
- When you add a new batch, change the last bio line to "New pieces just dropped ↓".

## 2. What to post (5 simple "buckets")
Never stare at a blank screen — just rotate through these:

| Bucket | Example |
|---|---|
| 🎬 **Process video** | Pouring resin, adding glitter, demolding a coaster |
| 💎 **Finished piece** | A clean photo of one product, named + priced-feel |
| 🙋 **You / behind-the-scenes** | Your workspace, packing an order, a blooper |
| ❤️ **Customer favourite** | "This one keeps selling out" / a repeat style |
| 📣 **Announcement** | New arrivals, a market you'll be at, glow reveal |

Aim for **3 posts a week** to start. Mostly Reels.

---

## 3. Reels (short video) — your #1 growth tool
Resin is *made* for video — people love watching it come together. Easy ideas:

1. **The reveal** — demolding a finished coaster ("watch this come to life 🌸").
2. **The pour** — resin swirling, glitter dropping in.
3. **Glow reveal** — show a piece in light, then lights off → it glows. 🌙
4. **Before → after** — raw supplies, then the finished piece.
5. **Slow glam pan** — a close-up glide across a sparkly pendant.
6. **"Pick one"** — line up 3 coasters, ask viewers to comment their favourite.

**How to make one (beginner steps)**
- Prop your phone up, film in **good light** (near a window is perfect), 10–20 seconds.
- In the Instagram app: tap **+** → **Reel** → add your clip(s).
- Add **trending audio** (tap the music note; pick a song with a little ↗ arrow = trending).
- The **first 1–2 seconds** must grab attention — start on the most eye-catching moment.
- Add a short caption + your hashtags (below), then **Share**.

Don't chase perfection. A slightly wobbly real video beats a perfect one you never post.

## 4. Caption templates (fill in the blanks)
Keep captions short, warm, and end with a **call to action**.

## 6. Stories (2 minutes a day, keeps followers warm)
Low-effort, disappears in 24h — perfect for casual stuff:
- A work-in-progress snap.
- A **poll**: "Which colour next? 💙 vs 💗"
- "Packing up an order 📦" (shows people are buying — builds trust).
- Re-share any post to your Story with "New today 👇".

---

## 7. Growing (gentle, no gimmicks)
- **Reply to every comment**, even just "🙏💕". Instagram rewards conversation.
- Spend 5 min after posting **liking/commenting** on other resin artists' and local makers' posts.
- Follow and cheer on **local BC makers / markets** — community shares community.
- **Don't** buy followers or use bots — it hurts your reach and trust.

---

## ✅ First Week Checklist (start here)
- [ ] Update your **bio** (section 1) and put **werecured.ca** in the website field.
- [ ] Pick **3 products** to feature this week.
- [ ] Film **one** short process or glow Reel (even 10 seconds).
- [ ] Post it with a caption + **Set A/B/C** hashtags and trending audio.
- [ ] Post **2 photos** of finished pieces on other days, with a caption + hashtags.
- [ ] Put up **1 Story** (a poll or a WIP snap).
- [ ] Reply to every comment you get.

Next week: repeat, and add one more Reel. That's the whole game — **consistency beats perfection.**
