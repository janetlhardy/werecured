# We're Cured! — Your First Month of Posts (ready to copy-paste)

A done-for-you **4-week plan**: 3 posts a week, each with a caption written for a real piece of yours.
Just film/photograph the piece, paste the caption, add the hashtag set it names, and post.

- Hashtag sets (**A** = coasters, **B** = jewellery, **C** = home décor / general) live in
  `INSTAGRAM_GUIDE.md` → section 5. Paste the whole block.
- 🎬 = film a short Reel (10–20s).  📷 = a photo or carousel of stills.
- Prices are optional to include — shown here so you can add or drop them.
- Swap in a different piece anytime; the captions are easy to reuse.
