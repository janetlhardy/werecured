# Boosting posts on Instagram and Facebook

A plain-language guide to your first ads. Written November 2026.

**The plan in one line:** boost one of your best posts for 7 days at **$5 a day**, shown to people
around **Victoria**, with a button sending them to **werecured.ca**. Then see what happened and
decide what to try next.

---

## Before your first boost (once only)

1. **Have a card ready.** Meta asks for a payment method the first time you boost. You type it in
   yourself on Meta's own screen. It's charged as the ad runs, never more than the budget you set.
2. **Pick the post.** The best ones to boost:
   - **A Reel (video), if you have one.** A pour, a drip bowl turning slowly in the light, or a glow-in-the-dark
     reveal. Video gets far more attention than still photos.
   - Otherwise, **your most striking single photo**: one piece, bright, filling the frame.
   - A post that already did well (more likes than usual) is a good sign.
   - ⚠️ **Avoid posts with popular music on them.** Meta often won't let you boost a Reel that
     uses a commercial song, **even one picked from Instagram's own music library** (found out
     November 2026: none of the existing reels could be boosted). Reels with no music, or music from
     Instagram's "original audio" or royalty-free sounds, are fine. Photo posts never have this
     problem.
   - **Making a reel you might want to boost later?** Pick its music by searching **"royalty free"** in
     the audio picker. Or make two versions: one with a trending song to post for free views, and
     a copy with royalty-free music to boost.
3. **Check the piece in the post is still for sale.** Sending people to something that's sold is
   wasted money.

## How to boost from the Instagram app

1. Open Instagram and go to your profile (**@were_cured**).
2. Tap the post or Reel you want, then tap the blue **Boost post** button under it.
3. **Goal:** choose **More website visits**.
   - Website: **https://werecured.ca**
   - Button: **Shop now**
   - Tip: you can send them straight to a category. Use **https://werecured.ca/#bowls** for a bowl
     post, **#earrings** for earrings, **#pendants**, **#coasters** or **#accents** for the others.
   - **Which link to use:**
     - A post or reel showing **several pieces** → **https://werecured.ca**, and tag those pieces
       **Featured** in Shopify first, so they're the first thing people see on the home page.
     - A post about **one piece** → a link straight to it, like
       **https://werecured.ca/#product/seafoam-agate-tray**. It opens that piece's photos and
       Add to Cart button, and closing it leaves them in its category, ready to browse. The end part
       is the piece's handle; ask Claude for it, or copy it from the bottom of the piece's page in
       Shopify (under "Search engine listing").
4. **Audience:** tap **Create your own**, then:
   - **Locations:** remove "Canada" if it's there, then search **Victoria, British Columbia** and set
     the circle to about **40 km**. That covers the whole Greater Victoria area and the Saanich
     Peninsula.
   - **Interests:** add a few, like *Handicraft*, *Interior design*, *Art* and *Gifts*. Don't add too many.
   - **Age:** 25 to 65+ is fine to start.
   - Name it **Victoria makers** so you can reuse it.
5. **Budget and duration:** **$5 a day** for **7 days** ($35 total).
6. **Facebook too?** If the screen offers to **also show the ad on Facebook**, leave it switched on.
   It costs no extra; Meta just spreads the same $5 across both. If you don't see that option, the
   ad runs on Instagram only, and that's fine.
7. Tap **Boost post**. Meta reviews it, usually within a few hours, then it starts.

## How to tell if it's working

After a few days, open the post and tap **View insights** (or **See results**). The numbers that matter:

| What it shows | What it means | A good sign |
|---|---|---|
| **Accounts reached** | how many people saw it | a few thousand for $35 is normal |
| **Link clicks** (or website visits) | people who tapped through to werecured.ca | anything under $1 each is good |
| **Cost per click** | money spent ÷ clicks | lower is better |

You can also see **sales** in Shopify. Watch for orders during and just after the boost.

**Don't judge it on likes.** Likes are nice but the point is people visiting the shop.

---

## After the first week

- **If clicks were cheap** (say under $1 each): boost another post the same way, or run the same
  one again.
- **If clicks were expensive or few:** try a different post, ideally a Reel. The picture matters more
  than anything else.
- **Wider area test:** once you've done Victoria, try one boost to **British Columbia and Alberta**
  and compare the cost per click. A wider area reaches more people, but every sale needs shipping,
  and heavy bowls cost more to send. Jewellery and coasters ship cheaply, so they suit the wider area best.
- **Christmas timing:** aim to have the last gift-season boost end by about **December 10**, so
  buyers outside Victoria can still get their parcel before Christmas.

You can **pause or stop a boost any time**: open the post, tap the boost results and choose
**Pause** or **Delete**. You're only charged for the days it actually ran.

## Your Facebook Page followers

You don't *have* to invite friends for ads to work. Ads reach strangers either way. But it's worth
doing once, because it's free and it helps:

- When an ad shows to someone, Meta can add "**3 of your friends follow We're Cured!**", and people
  trust that.
- Friends who follow see your new pieces without you paying.
- A Page with a handful of followers looks more established than one with none.

**How:** on Facebook, switch to the We're Cured! Page, tap the **⋯** menu (or Page settings) and
choose **Invite friends**. Invite whoever you like. Local friends and anyone who's ever admired your
work are the best ones. You can invite them all at once. People who aren't interested just ignore it.
