# The plan

Ten weeks out is plenty — you don't need to post about the show the whole time. **Weeks 1–6 carry
on as normal** with `FIRST_MONTH_POSTS.md`. The countdown proper starts about four weeks out.

The one job before then: **the pendant group photo**, whenever it's convenient. It's the strongest
image you'll have and two posts below depend on it.

## At the table

Two things that cost nothing and do more than any single post:

- **A small sign with `@were_cured` and `werecured.ca` on it.** Most people who stop won't buy, but
  a good number will follow if you make it effortless. Right now the account has 59 followers — a
  busy show weekend can move that more than months of posting.
- **The URL on every bag, card and sticker.** A show sale ends when they walk away; a website
  address in the bag is what brings them back in December.

## If someone asks for a piece that's sold

It'll happen — everything is one-of-a-kind. Best answer:

> "That one's gone I'm afraid, but I put new pieces up at werecured.ca all the time — worth a
> follow on Instagram, I post them there first."

Turns a dead end into a follower.
