Booth banner — options

All are 72″ × 24″ (6ft × 2ft), shown here scaled down. The dashed line is the safe area — nothing important crosses it, so hems and grommets can't eat any text.

You leaned towards Option 1. It is shown three times below at different brightness, because the darkness came from a dark scrim laid over the photo to keep the lettering readable — that scrim is what gets dialled back. Pick 1A, 1B or 1C. Options 2 and 3 are still here underneath if you change your mind.

No QR code on the banner, on purpose — a code hanging behind your table is too far away for phones to read. The QR lives on the table sign (table-sign.html), where people can reach it.

To make the print file: File → Print → Destination Save as PDF → Paper size 72 × 24 in (or “Custom”) → Margins None → tick Background graphics → Save. If the printer asks for bleed, tell them to add 0.25″ each side — there's plenty of colour to pull from.

Photo note — the photo is now 1890 × 2048. That is the 2048-pixel working copy from your werecured-product-photos folder, swapped in on 2026-09-06 to replace the 1384-pixel one. Across a 6ft banner, zoomed to 130% to keep the bare table out of shot, it works out to about 20 dots per inch — up from 15, but still under the 30 dots per inch that large-format printers normally want.

This is not the camera original. A photo straight off the phone is usually around 3000–4000 pixels wide, which would land near 32–42 dots per inch and settle the question. To get it: open the photo in Photos, then File → Export → Export Unmodified Original — the plain Export command re-compresses to a smaller size, which is how these 2048-pixel copies were made in the first place.

How much does 20 dpi matter? Less than it sounds, for this particular picture. There is no text or fine detail in it — it is soft organic colour, which is the forgiving case. From across an aisle it will read as rich colour. Standing right at the table, it will look slightly soft. Option 3 uses no photo at all and is perfectly sharp if that bothers you.
