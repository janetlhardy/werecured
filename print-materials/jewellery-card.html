Jewellery card — stands 5″ × 3.5″

For the pendants, earrings and anything else at the show that isn't listed on the website. Stand it beside those pieces.

It leads with the dried flowers because that is the honest reason someone should buy it today rather than think about it: you pick and dry them yourself, and once this year's are used up there are no more until they grow again. That is real scarcity, not a sales line — and it is a nicer thing to read than “buy now”.

It's a tent card. Print it, trim to 5 × 7″, then fold across the middle (the yellow dashed line) so it stands on its own. The top half is printed upside down on purpose — that is what makes the back read the right way up once folded.

To print at home: File → Print → Paper Letter → Scale Fit to page → tick Background graphics → print on card stock, then trim and fold. Print two or three — one for the pendants, one for the earrings.
