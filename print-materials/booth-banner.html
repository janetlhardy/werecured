Booth banner — 72″ × 24″ (6ft × 2ft)

Shown here scaled down. The dashed line is the safe area — nothing important crosses it, so hems and grommets can't eat any text.

There is no QR code on this banner on purpose. Hung behind your table it would be too far away for phones to focus on. The QR lives on the table sign (table-sign.html), within arm's reach — print the photo background version, which matches this banner.

To make the print file: File → Print → Destination Save as PDF → Paper size 72 × 24 in (or “Custom”) → Margins None → tick Background graphics → Save. That PDF goes to the printer.

If your printer asks for bleed, tell them to add 0.25″ on each side — there's plenty of background colour to pull from.

The background is a wide slice of your Ocean Daisy coaster, cut from the full-size camera photo and taken from below the flower's dark centre so it doesn't sit behind the name. It lives at images/banner-background.jpeg and is used only by this banner.
