Price signs — 8½″ × 11″

Two table signs for the November show, made to match the dark QR table sign: one for the Spilled Wine and Spilled Coffee on the back table, one for the wine bottle stoppers. Letter size, for the 8½ × 11 acrylic holders.

The coloured dots on the stopper sign work either way: as a key if you put matching dot stickers on the bottoms, or just as decoration if you group the stoppers by price.

To print at home: open price-signs.pdf → File → Print → Paper Letter → Scale Fit to page → print on card stock or plain paper. Most home printers leave a thin white edge; the holder's frame hides it, or trim it off if you like.
